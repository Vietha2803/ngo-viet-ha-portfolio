Overview

Lang Son cleared 521,931 trucks in the first 293 days of 2026, about 1,781 a day; 7 in 10 carried imports. Road moves 75.8% of Vietnam's freight tonnage (NSO, 10M 2026).

Findings

Tet is the only real capacity break. 773 trucks a day from 12 Feb to 1 Mar, against about 1,880 in a normal period.

The queue follows fruit, not volume. Fruit is 40 to 86% of trucks left waiting; the worst day (1 Mar) was a low volume day.

Coc Nam is the only export led gate (57% export).

Actions

Move Tet volume into late January, when 2,004 trucks a day cleared. Send customers a delay update once queue pressure passes 20%. Pair export loads with import pickups to cut empty kilometres.

Overview

14,787 real Delhivery trips (12 Sep to 3 Oct 2018), rebuilt from 26,222 segments. A trip took 353 minutes against 181 planned on average, so only 1 in 5 finished within 1.5× plan. A third of scan to scan time was spent at hubs.

Findings

Short hops are least reliable: under 25 km only 12% are on plan.

Bhiwandi and Mumbai (707 trips) are on plan 2 to 3% of the time; Maharashtra 5.6% vs Karnataka 35.3%.

Dispatch time matters: 31% on plan at 20:00, 9% at 04:00.

Actions

Plan carting runs with a fixed handling allowance, not pure drive time. Start a root cause review at the Bhiwandi hub on dock slots and scan time. Move flexible departures from 02:00 to 07:00 into 19:00 to 22:00.
