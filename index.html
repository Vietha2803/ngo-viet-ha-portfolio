Case 01 · Independent project · Nov 2026

Road Freight Operations Report

Solo · data, KPIs and dashboard

Where do trucks lose time at the Lang Son border and on long haul trucking routes, and what should a planner do about it?

  • Collected 2026 Lang Son border gate bulletins and NSO freight statistics. Vietnam publishes no trip level data, so I rebuilt 14,787 real Delhivery trips in India from 26,222 segments in Python.
  • Defined planner KPIs: customs queue pressure with 10% and 20% limits at the border, plus on plan rate against the route plan and hours over plan for the trips.
  • Built a daily control report for the border and a daily truck tracker comparing planned and actual time per truck, with status rules that flag which customers need a delay update, plus a loading plan table.

Case 02 · CBS Global Case Competition 2026

ROCKWOOL: right size on the right day

Case team · delivery and supply chain design

Contractors in Denmark drop ROCKWOOL insulation after the tender when deliveries arrive late or incomplete. How can the supply chain become the reason they choose it?

  • Mapped the contractor journey: delivery and stock were the top pain point, because a missing size on site costs more in idle labour than the price difference.
  • Designed the delivery model: standard sizes from distributor stock, special sizes made at the Danish factories and sent direct to site, site demand updated every two weeks, every delivery tracked and shortages or surplus fixed.
  • Planned disruption fallbacks: nearest distributor stock, a factory rush run for urgent sizes, an approved equivalent product for major disruption, plus Rockcycle pickup of site waste.
  • Linked quote, order and delivery under one project ID, so distributors stock from confirmed projects and on time in full is tracked per project.

Economist Up 2026

Team leader · inventory and export strategy

A coffee exporter selling 40–45% of exports to the U.S. Found a surplus yet shortage problem: about 5,000 tonnes of substandard stock while up to 97% of raw material was bought in (2023), and wet processing use fell from 36% to 16%.

Traced it to fragmented farm supply and weak conversion of stock into orders. Proposed routing B grade stock to domestic, ASEAN and China buyers and OEM orders on idle capacity, plus a shift toward instant and roasted coffee worth 2.6× to 5.9× green beans.

Tsinghua Global SDG Case 2026

Team leader · survey and policy design

Social insurance for Vietnam's 690,000 platform drivers. Surveyed 229 drivers and tested the results in Stata: 86% of drivers with an incident paid the cost themselves; 78% prefer income adjusted contributions.

Compared 3 policy options against 5 design principles and recommended a tiered model with a pilot roadmap in Ho Chi Minh City.
